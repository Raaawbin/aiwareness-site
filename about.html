About

Awareness for the age of AI

AIwareness exists to bring conscious attention to the culture, leadership, and decisions behind the technology reshaping our world.

Our vision

A conscious culture and a healthy human future

A future where people and the technology they build grow together. We believe the systems reshaping humanity should be created with awareness, not on autopilot, so that progress serves human flourishing rather than quietly eroding the things that make us human.

Our mission

Bring awareness into AI

Today’s pace of AI puts that future at risk if we don’t steer the technology well. So we bring awareness into AI through education and coaching, for teams, for teens, and for society, working from the Cocreation Loft in Berlin, a community centred on human flourishing.

Founder

Meet Robin, founder of AIwareness

Hey, I’m Robin. I live in Berlin, and for over ten years I worked as a visual facilitator, sketching the ideas and strategies of founders and teams, and helping people step back to see the bigger picture.

I loved that work, but I kept looking for a deeper sense of purpose. I found it when I started paying real attention to AI: the wonder of it, but also how fast it’s moving, and how far public awareness lags behind what these systems can already do.

That’s why I’m running AIwareness, where the human-centred work I’ve always loved meets the conviction that we need to face AI together, with both curiosity and care. And I’m not doing it alone: behind me stands a team of excellent facilitators who make this work possible.
