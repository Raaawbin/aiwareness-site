About

Awareness for the age of AI

AIwareness exists to bring conscious attention to the culture, leadership, and decisions behind the technology reshaping our world.

Purpose

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

A conscious culture and a healthy human future, where people and the technology they build grow together. We believe the systems reshaping humanity should be created with awareness, not on autopilot, so that progress serves human flourishing rather than quietly eroding the things that make us human.

Our Mission

Today's pace of AI puts that future at risk if we don't steer the technology well. So we bring awareness into AI through education and coaching, for teams, for teens, and for society, working from the Cocreation Loft in Berlin, a community centred on human flourishing.

Founder

Meet Robin, founder of AIwareness

I'm Robin. For over ten years I worked as a Visual Facilitator, sketching the ideas and strategies of founders and organisations, and helping people step back to see the bigger picture.

I loved that work, but I kept searching for a deeper sense of purpose. I found it when I learned about the dangers of AI, and saw how far public awareness lags behind what these systems can already do.

AIwareness is where my two threads meet: the visual, human-centred work I've always loved, and the conviction that we need to face AI together, with both wonder and care.
