The format

Three parts, one shared evening

No hype, no doom, no expert panel telling you what to think. Just space to slow down, think together and ask the big questions about AI and our place in it.

PART 01

Input

A bit of input to get us all on the same page about where AI is heading and why it matters.

PART 02

Hands-on

A hands-on piece, so you experience the questions instead of only hearing about them.

PART 03

Sense-making circle

We make sense of it together, and you walk away knowing a little better where you actually stand.
