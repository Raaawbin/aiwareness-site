Event

AI & Human Agency

A sense-making evening to connect, learn, and find your own relationship to AI — in Berlin, 30 June 2026.

WhenTuesday, 30 June 2026 · 18:00–21:00
Wherecocreation.loft · Schinkestraße 9, 12047 Berlin, Germany
Register

How do we make sense of Artificial Intelligence?

  • What is the good that the technology is capable of?
  • What is the harm that it could do?
  • What becomes possible with it that wasn’t before?

These questions are increasingly important to ask, and to engage in collective sense-making around, as Artificial Intelligence continues to develop rapidly and create more impact on human social systems.

This event will be part education, part hands-on experience, and part sense-making circle, as we gather together to connect, learn, and find our own relationship to AI while the technology becomes more and more a part of the global conversation.

Join your hosts Johanna Schuck, Robin Hotz, and JJ Vega as they facilitate an evening where you can come as you are: optimistic, pessimistic, excited, concerned, scared, curious, and everything in between.

You will walk away clearer about where you stand with AI, and how you want to move forward with your relationship to the technology.
