Privacy Policy

How your data is handled on this website, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO).

1. Overview & controller

Protecting your personal data matters to us. This policy explains what personal data is collected when you visit this website, how it is used, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

2. Hosting & server log files

This website is hosted by STRATO AG (Germany). When you visit the site, the hosting provider’s servers automatically collect and store information that your browser transmits, in so-called server log files:

  • browser type and version
  • operating system used
  • referrer URL (the previously visited page)
  • the requested page and time of the server request
  • your IP address (typically shortened or anonymized)

This data is not merged with other data sources and is processed to ensure a secure, stable, and technically functional website. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the proper operation and security of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Hosting is carried out by STRATO AG as a processor on our behalf, on the basis of a data processing agreement (Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag) pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

3. Contact form & email contact

If you use the contact form, the details you enter — your name, email address, company and role, your described challenge, and any optional message — are transmitted to us by email so we can respond to your enquiry. We do not use this data for any other purpose without your consent, and we do not share it with third parties beyond what is necessary to deliver the message (our hosting provider).

If you contact us directly by email, the information you provide will be stored for the purpose of handling your request and any follow-up questions.

The legal basis for processing this data is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a (potential) contract, and otherwise our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is retained until your request has been fully dealt with, and thereafter only as long as required by statutory retention obligations. You may ask us to delete it at any time (see your rights below).

4. Web fonts

To present its content consistently, this website currently uses Google Fonts, provided by Google Ireland Limited. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google’s servers to retrieve the fonts, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google and may be processed on servers in the USA. This is based on our legitimate interest in a uniform and appealing presentation of our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Further information is available in Google’s privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Newsletter sign-up (Substack)

Our newsletter is hosted by Substack, Inc. (USA). Some pages of this website (Home, Newsletter, Events and our newsletter notes) contain Substack's sign-up form, which is loaded directly from Substack's servers when that part of the page comes into view. Your browser then transmits technical data such as your IP address and browser information to Substack, and Substack may set its own cookies. If you subscribe, the email address you enter is sent directly to Substack, which manages your subscription and delivers the newsletter.

We embed the form based on our legitimate interest in offering a simple way to subscribe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); the subscription itself is based on your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. Substack may process data in the USA; its own privacy policy applies: https://substack.com/privacy.

6. Cookies & analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services. Any storage used is strictly technical and limited to what is necessary for the website to function. Pages that contain the newsletter sign-up form load it from Substack, which may set its own cookies (see section 5).

7. SSL / TLS encryption

For security, this site uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognize an encrypted connection by the “https://” prefix and the padlock symbol in your browser’s address bar. When encryption is active, the data you transmit to us cannot be read by third parties.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data, free of charge:

  • Access (Art. 15) — to know whether and what data we process about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — to have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — to have your data deleted, where no legal obligation requires its retention.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) — to restrict processing in certain cases.
  • Data portability (Art. 20) — to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Art. 21) — to object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe the processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The authority responsible for the controller is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg): www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes to the website or to legal requirements. The version published here always applies.
