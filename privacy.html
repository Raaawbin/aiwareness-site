3. Contact form & email contact

When you send the contact form, your details (name, email address, optional company and role, topic and message) are transmitted over an encrypted connection to a small script on the servers of our web host STRATO AG (Germany), which forwards them to us as an email to hello@aiwareness.space. Like any web server, it records technical data of the request, such as your IP address, in its log files. Nothing is stored on this website itself. STRATO also runs our email inbox and processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). If sending fails, you can open your own email program with your details filled in instead; only when you send that email does it reach us.

If you contact us by email, we store the information you provide in order to handle your request and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a (potential) contract, and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep the data until your request has been dealt with, and after that only as long as statutory retention obligations require. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see your rights below).

Our newsletter is hosted by Substack, Inc. (USA). Some pages of this website (Home, Newsletter, Events and our newsletter notes) contain Substack’s sign-up form, which is loaded directly from Substack’s servers when that part of the page comes into view. 6. Cookies & local storage

This website does not set tracking cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking services. To remember your language choice (English or German), the website saves one small entry in your browser’s local storage (“aiw_lang”). It stays on your device, is not sent to us and is strictly necessary for the function you chose (§ 25(2) TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings. Pages that contain the newsletter sign-up form load it from Substack, which may set its own cookies (see section 5).

6. Cookies & lokaler Speicher

Diese Website setzt keine Tracking-Cookies und nutzt keine Analyse-, Werbe- oder Tracking-Dienste Dritter. Um Deine Sprachwahl (Englisch oder Deutsch) zu merken, legt die Website einen kleinen Eintrag im lokalen Speicher Deines Browsers ab („aiw_lang“). Er bleibt auf Deinem Gerät, wird nicht an uns übertragen und ist für die von Dir gewählte Funktion unbedingt erforderlich (§ 25 Abs. 2 TDDDG). 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data, free of charge:

  • Access (Art. 15): to know whether and what data we process about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): to have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): to have your data deleted, where no legal obligation requires its retention.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): to restrict processing in certain cases.
  • Data portability (Art. 20): to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Art. 21): to object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1.

8. Deine Rechte

Nach der DSGVO hast Du bezüglich Deiner personenbezogenen Daten kostenlos folgende Rechte:

  • Auskunft (Art. 15): zu erfahren, ob und welche Daten wir über Dich verarbeiten.
  • Berichtigung (Art. 16): unrichtige Daten korrigieren zu lassen.
  • Löschung (Art. 17): Deine Daten löschen zu lassen, soweit keine gesetzliche Pflicht zur Aufbewahrung besteht.
  • Einschränkung (Art. 18): die Verarbeitung in bestimmten Fällen einschränken zu lassen.
  • Datenübertragbarkeit (Art. 20): Deine Daten in einem strukturierten, maschinenlesbaren Format zu erhalten.
  • Widerspruch (Art. 21): einer Verarbeitung auf Grundlage berechtigter Interessen zu widersprechen.
  • Widerruf einer Einwilligung: Beruht eine Verarbeitung auf Deiner Einwilligung, kannst Du sie jederzeit mit Wirkung für die Zukunft widerrufen.

Um diese Rechte auszuüben, wende Dich an die Kontaktdaten in Abschnitt 1.
